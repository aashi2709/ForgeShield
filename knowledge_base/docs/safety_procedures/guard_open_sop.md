## Purpose

This procedure defines the recommended response when a machine guard is detected as open or improperly secured.

## Detection Indicators

A guard-open condition may be indicated by:

- Guard interlock activation
- Guard-position sensor alert
- Visible open or unsecured guard
- Unexpected machine access condition

## Immediate Response

When a guard-open condition is detected:

1. Do not continue operation with the guard in an unsafe condition.
2. Keep personnel clear of hazardous machine areas.
3. Follow the applicable machine shutdown or isolation procedure.
4. Inspect the guard and associated interlock system.
5. Resume operation only after the required safety conditions have been restored.

## Possible Contributing Factors

Potential contributing factors include:

- Improperly secured guard
- Guard damage
- Interlock malfunction
- Sensor failure
- Maintenance activity

These factors are possible explanations and require inspection for confirmation.

## Preventive Measures

Recommended preventive measures include:

- Inspect machine guards regularly.
- Test guard interlocks.
- Maintain guard-position sensors.
- Include safety guards in preventive-maintenance inspections.
