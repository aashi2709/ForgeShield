## Purpose

This procedure defines the recommended response when a potential gas leakage condition is detected.

## Detection Indicators

Potential gas leakage may be indicated by:

- Gas detection system alerts
- Abnormal gas concentration readings
- Unexpected pressure changes
- Suspected leakage from pipes, hoses, seals, or connections
- Inadequate ventilation

## Immediate Response

When gas leakage is suspected:

1. Follow the applicable emergency shutdown procedure.
2. Restrict access to the affected area.
3. Follow the site's established emergency response procedure.
4. Inspect the suspected leakage source only when this can be performed safely.
5. Verify that the atmosphere is safe before resuming operation.

## Possible Contributing Factors

Potential contributing factors include:

- Damaged pipes or hoses
- Failed seals
- Loose connections
- Equipment malfunction
- Insufficient ventilation

These are possible explanations and must not be treated as confirmed root causes without further investigation.

## Preventive Measures

Recommended preventive measures include:

- Inspect gas-handling components regularly.
- Test gas detection equipment.
- Maintain ventilation systems.
- Review emergency response procedures.
